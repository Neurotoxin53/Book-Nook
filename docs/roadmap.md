# My Book Nook release roadmap

## 0.2.0 — Foundation

Rename, release automation, CI, changelog, version display, and design brief.

## 0.3.0 — Passkeys and durable data

Invitation-only passkey accounts, D1 persistence, recovery keys, protected
library APIs, and confirmed migration of real device-local books.

## 0.4.0 — Shelf and metadata

Minimal cover grid, mobile baseline, and editable Open Library lookup with
field provenance and manual fallback.

## 0.5.0 — Goodreads migration

Local CSV parsing, preview, conflict handling, idempotent import, reporting,
and undo.

## 0.6.0 — Appearance and genres

Versioned appearance registries, 36 self-hosted Google Fonts, 8 constructions,
10 scenes, 6 page treatments, 25 genres, 10 facets, and explainable templates.

## 0.7.0 — Private beta

Passkey/recovery hardening, PWA metadata, app preview, accessibility, and real
iPhone acceptance testing.

## 0.9.0 — Domain migration

Purchase and attach the final domain only after beta stability, then bridge
existing accounts through one-time transfer tokens and new-domain passkey
enrollment. The old Sites hostname remains available for roughly 90 days.
