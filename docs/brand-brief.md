# My Book Nook — graphic-design brief

## Product idea

My Book Nook is a private digital reading journal. The outer library is quiet,
minimal, and cover-first; opening a title reveals a tactile, customizable book
and scrapbook-like reading memory.

## Personality

Warm, intimate, literate, collected, slightly magical, and never childish.
The identity should support both a clean contemporary shelf and richly themed
book interiors.

## Requested deliverables

- Primary wordmark and a compact mark that remains recognizable at 16–32 px.
- Light and dark variants plus one-color artwork.
- Core palette with accessible text/background pairings.
- Brand typography recommendations for UI, editorial text, and display moments.
- App icon master plus iOS, PWA, and favicon exports.
- 1200×630 social-preview composition with a safe text area.
- A small set of optional paper, stamp, or ex-libris motifs for book interiors.
- Source files, SVG/PNG exports, usage rules, and font/license notes.

## Guardrails

- Keep the home screen visually calm; do not make the logo itself a collage.
- Avoid literal house-and-book clip art and overly ornate faux-antique lettering.
- The mark must work without the words “My Book Nook.”
- Decorative typography is for headings only; review text must remain readable.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast for any supplied digital color combinations.

## Current placeholder direction

Deep ink, warm paper, oxblood, sage, and brass. The product uses a simple open
book/ex-libris mark until final artwork arrives.
